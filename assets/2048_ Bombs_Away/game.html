Hello and welcome to 2048: Bomb's Away

Here is how to play the game:

  • Use the arrow buttons or the arrow keys to shift tiles
  • Tiles of the same number will combine
  • One time during each game, you can detonate a bomb
  • Detonate a bomb by pressing 'b' or the bomb button
  • The bomb explodes in a plus shape around the selected tile
  • Tiles blown up by bombs DO NOT contribute to your score
  • Try to get as many points as possible by getting larger tiles
  • If you can't combine any more tiles, you lose!
